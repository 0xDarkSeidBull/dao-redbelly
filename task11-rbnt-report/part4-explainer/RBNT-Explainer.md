# What Actually Backs RBNT? A Quick Look at Redbelly's Token

Every blockchain token needs a job. For RBNT, the native token of
Redbelly Network, that job is running an entire compliance-first,
real-world-asset blockchain — not just paying gas.

**Supply is fixed.** There are 10,000,000,000 RBNT, full stop. No
inflation, no ongoing minting, no burn mechanism written into the
protocol. Whatever exists today is what will exist later.

**Five real uses, straight from the whitepaper:**

- **Gas** — every transaction on Redbelly needs RBNT to run.
- **Governance** — RBNT holders vote on network upgrades, allocation
  changes, and validator-set reconfiguration.
- **Staking** — three separate flavors: consensus staking, SEVM
  staking, and oracle staking, each earning rewards for securing a
  different part of the network.
- **Sharding** — participants lock RBNT to initiate and manage shards,
  Redbelly's approach to scaling.
- **Incentives & rewards** — node operators, oracle providers, and
  ecosystem builders all get paid in RBNT for growing the network.

**Where the 10B actually goes:**

| Allocation | Share |
|---|---|
| Ecosystem Development | 37% |
| Investors (Seed + Private Sale A/B/C) | 28% |
| Reserve | 20% |
| Team | 10% |
| Governance DAO | 3% |
| University of Sydney & CSIRO | 2% |

Release is staggered, not a cliff — the Governance DAO's allocation
starts unlocking in month 1, Team tokens don't start releasing until
month 12 and only while actively engaged with Redbelly, and most
buckets stretch out to month 32–36. Ecosystem Development and Reserve
don't follow a fixed schedule at all — they're released as needed, at
the network's discretion.

**Why fixed gas pricing matters.** Redbelly prices gas at a flat
$0.000000476190476 per unit — a standard transfer (21,000 gas) costs
about a cent, in dollar terms, regardless of what RBNT itself is doing
in the market. That's a deliberate design choice for an RWA chain:
institutions moving real assets need predictable costs, not gas fees
that spike with speculation.

**Governance today, governance tomorrow.** Right now, Redbelly Network
Pty Ltd (an Australia-registered entity) administers governance
alongside the not-for-profit Redbelly Network Governance DAO, as the
network works toward full DAO-led decision-making over time.

**What this isn't:** a price prediction. Redbelly's whitepaper doesn't
publish a valuation model, and neither will we — RBNT's utility is in
what it lets the network *do* (secure consensus, gate governance, price
gas, fund incentives), not in a number someone else assigns it later.
